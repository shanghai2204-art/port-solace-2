**Port Solace** is an original top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser — no build step, no libraries, no network. You are Rook, a dock courier turned wheelman in a seeded coastal city of six districts (Old Town, Downtown, The Flats, Harbor, Garden Row, Airstrip Flats) with lane-following traffic, sidewalk pedestrians, stealable cars, on-foot gunplay, and a five-star wanted system whose police pursue, intercept and bust you. Between free-roam mayhem, fixer Mara Voss hands out five story missions from her harbor cafe — a first delivery, a steal-and-drop, a checkpoint run against the clock, an escort under police pressure, and a finale that asks you to lose the whole force. Cash and completed jobs persist in localStorage; all sound is synthesized live with WebAudio.

How to play: **WASD / arrows** walk and drive · **F** enter or exit a car (walk up to a driven car to jack it) · **E** take the next job at Mara's cafe · **mouse** aims, **click** shoots (on foot only) · **Shift** sprint · **Space** handbrake · **P / Esc** pause · **M** mute · **N** on the title screen starts a new game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8902
```
